Signs it's time to call

Catching these early can save you from a bigger repair, or a breakdown on the hottest or coldest day of the year.

Unusual noises

Clanking, banging, or groaning from your furnace or air conditioner.

Weak airflow

Less air coming from the vents than there used to be.

Rising energy bills

A sharp jump in costs often means your system is working harder than it should.

Wrong temperatures

An AC that won't cool or a furnace that won't heat the way it used to.
