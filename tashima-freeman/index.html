About Tashima

My work starts with the numbers and ends with people: owners who can finally read their books, professionals who leave corporate with a plan, and neighbors who get a seat at the table.

I'm an accountant by training, with a bachelor's degree in accounting and an MBA in management. I spent my early career as an accountant, senior accountant, and financial analyst, then moved into production accounting for film, television, and music projects.

In 2020 I turned a hard season into KSK Tax & Accounting Solutions, and reached six figures within nine months. A year later I started coaching professionals who wanted out of corporate, which grew into Elite Circle.

I'm raising my children in South Fulton, and I'm a Muslim woman whose faith shapes how I show up for my clients and my community.

For South Fulton

In 2025 I ran for City Council in District 6. The campaign is over, but the priorities haven't changed, and I'm still working on them with neighbors, organizations, and local businesses.

Youth and families

Free transportation, a makers space where young people learn real skills, and a free college tour program.

Open city finances

Budgets residents can read, clearer communication from City Hall, and full community access to city planning.

Homes and elders

A home repair support program, and Adopt a Granny, which pairs young people with the seniors on their street.

Safer streets

More streetlights where crime is highest, and a mental health crisis team that can de-escalate 911 calls.

Partner on a project

Faith and family

Before any title, I'm a mother. My faith as a Muslim woman keeps me grounded, honest with my clients, and committed to serving the people around me. Both are the reason I do this work in the city where my children are growing up.
